summary: See every change to platforms — creation, edits, configuration, status, mappings, documents and notes — with old and new values.
=== what
Every change to a platform is written to an insert-only history: creation, edits, configuration and capability changes, settings, status changes, archiving and restoring, GST, business, partner and warehouse mappings, document uploads, views, downloads and archiving, and notes.
=== who
**platforms.view_history**. You only see the history of platforms you are allowed to see.
=== step: History of one platform | ec-history
Open the platform's **History** tab. Each row shows the event, old and new values, the reason, the user and the time. The audit log underneath lists the same actions from the system-wide activity log.
=== step: History of all platforms | ec-history-all
**E-commerce → Platform History** lists changes across all visible platforms. Filter by platform, event and dates.
=== result
You can trace who changed what, when and why.
=== notes
- History entries are never edited or deleted.
- Partner mapping details are hidden from users without access to the partner-mappings field.
