summary: Link the warehouses that fulfil a platform's orders, including marketplace fulfilment centres.
=== what
Warehouse mappings record which Phase 7 warehouses dispatch orders for the platform — your own warehouses and marketplace fulfilment centres alike.
=== who
**platforms.manage_warehouse_mappings**, plus Inventory access to choose warehouses. Only warehouses you can see in Inventory are offered.
=== step: Add or end a link | ec-warehouses
Open the **Warehouse mappings** tab, choose a warehouse, the start date and an optional note, and click **Add**. Use **End link** with a reason when a warehouse stops serving the platform.
=== result
The warehouse counts toward the configuration checklist and the dashboard's linked records.
=== notes
- Several warehouses can serve one platform, and one warehouse can serve several platforms.
- Stock sync with the platform is not available yet; stock still moves only through Inventory documents.
- Archived warehouses cannot be added.
