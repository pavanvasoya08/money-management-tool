summary: Add a marketplace, online store or other channel with its code, category, website, country, currency and logo, then edit it later.
=== what
Each sales channel is added once as a platform. The same record is later used by seller accounts (Phase 8B), product listings, orders and settlements.
=== who
Adding needs **platforms.create**; editing **platforms.edit**.
=== prerequisites
The category must exist and be active (see *Managing platform categories*).
=== step: Fill in the form | ec-create
Click **Add platform**. Enter the **platform name** and choose the **category**. Leave **Platform code** empty to generate it from the name (e.g. *AMAZONINDIA*), or enter your own: capital letters, digits, dashes or underscores. Add the website, country, default currency, a description and the display order. Optionally upload a **logo** (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB).
=== step: Continue on the platform page | ec-config
After saving you land on the **Configuration** tab to complete the setup.
=== result
The platform is saved as *Under configuration* with every capability marked *Not known* and *Not available*.
=== notes
- Names and codes must be unique. Once product prices use the code as their sales channel, the code can no longer change.
- Internal notes are visible only to users allowed to see that field.
=== mistakes
- Do **not** type seller-portal passwords, API keys or tokens into the description or notes. Secure credential storage comes with seller accounts in Phase 8B.
