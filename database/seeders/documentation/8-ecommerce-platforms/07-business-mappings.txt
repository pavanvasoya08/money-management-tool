summary: Set the default business currency and link business activities and partners to a platform as permitted scopes.
=== what
The **Business mappings** tab holds the currency the business records this channel in, the business activities (lines of business) the platform is used for, and the partners permitted to work with it.
=== who
**platforms.manage_business_mappings**. Partner mappings are a sensitive field: users without access to it do not see them.
=== step: Currency, activities and partners | ec-business
Choose the **default business currency** and click **Save**. Under **Business activities** and **Partners**, choose a record, the start date and an optional note, then click **Add**. End a link with a date and reason.
=== result
The links are recorded with effective dates and appear in the history.
=== notes
- A partner mapping is **only a scope**. It creates no ownership, capital or profit-sharing rights, and no profit-sharing percentage is derived from it.
- Business activities and partners are the existing records from Phases 3 and 4; nothing is duplicated.
