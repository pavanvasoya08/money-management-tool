summary: Use the standard categories or add your own, such as Quick commerce, and deactivate the ones you do not use.
=== what
Categories describe the kind of sales channel. Seven are provided — Marketplace, E-commerce store, Social commerce, B2B platform, Own website, Offline channel and Other — and you can add more.
=== who
Anyone who can view platforms sees the list; adding and editing needs **platforms.manage_categories**.
=== step: Add or edit a category | ec-categories
Go to **E-commerce → Platform Categories**. Enter a name (the code is generated when empty), an optional description and the order. Click **Edit** on a row to rename it or switch it off.
=== result
Active categories appear in the platform form and in every category filter.
=== notes
- A category used by a platform that is not archived cannot be deactivated — move those platforms first.
- The codes of the standard categories do not change.
- The page also lists the platform capabilities and the phase that will implement each.
