=== what
Every product has a permanent product code (PRD-000001) and a stable identifier for integrations. The SKU is the code your team and marketplaces use; variants have their own SKUs.
=== who
Changing a SKU needs **products.manage_sku**; the patterns and SKU rules are in **Product settings** (**products.manage_settings**).
=== step: Choose the SKU rules | product-settings
In **Product Management → Settings → Rules**, set the product pattern (default {CAT}-{SEQ:5}) and the variant pattern (default {PARENT}-{SEQ:2}). Tokens: {CAT} category code, {BRAND} brand code, {TYPE} product type, {NAME} first letters of the name, {YYYY}/{YY} year, {PARENT} the product SKU and {SEQ:n} a running number with n digits. You can switch off automatic SKUs or typing SKUs by hand.
=== step: See and change a SKU | product-profile
The SKU is on the product card. To change it, use **Change SKU** on the Basic information tab (or **SKU** on a variant row) with a reason. When the *SKU change* workflow is on, the change waits for approval.
=== result
Each SKU is unique across all products and variants. A SKU that was used once stays reserved, even after it was changed or the product was archived.
=== notes
- Each filled-in prefix has its own running number: KIT-00001, KIT-00002 and GEN-00001 can exist side by side. Numbers already taken by typed SKUs are skipped.
- SKUs are stored in capitals without spaces; "kit-00003 " becomes KIT-00003.
- EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A and GTIN-14 barcodes are checked for their check digit; every barcode must be unique.
=== mistakes
- Reusing an old SKU for a new product — the system refuses it so old orders and stock records stay unambiguous.
