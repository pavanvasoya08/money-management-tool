=== what
The **Images & documents** tab holds product photos (optionally for one variant) and documents such as specification sheets, manuals, certificates, warranty and compliance documents.
=== who
Uploading and archiving needs **products.manage_images** or **products.manage_documents**; opening documents needs **products.download_documents**.
=== step: Upload images | product-media-tab
Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image (up to 5 MB), optionally a variant and a caption, and upload. The first image becomes the primary image shown in lists and on the product card; use **Make primary** on another image to change it.
=== step: Upload documents | product-media-tab
Under documents choose the file, its category, a title and an optional validity date and upload. Use **View** or **Download** to open it.
=== result
Images appear in the gallery and in lists; documents are listed with who uploaded them and when.
=== notes
- Files are stored privately outside the public web folder and are only served after a permission check. Images are checked by their content, not just their file name.
- Archive an image or document with a reason instead of deleting it; archived files stay in the history.
- Every document view and download is logged.
=== mistakes
- Uploading a supplier's bank proof here — supplier documents belong on the supplier's Documents tab, where bank proofs are restricted.
