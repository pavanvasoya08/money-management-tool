=== what
The Product Dashboard summarises the products you are allowed to see: how many are active, inactive, discontinued, waiting for approval, drafts or archived, how many variants exist, which products still miss a SKU, HSN/SAC code, GST set-up or supplier, and how products spread over categories.
=== why
It shows quickly where the catalogue needs work before products are used in purchases, inventory and sales in later phases.
=== who
Anyone with **products.view_dashboard**. Figures follow your record scope, so an accountant with assigned products only sees those.
=== step: Open the dashboard and filter | product-dashboard
Go to **Product Management → Product Dashboard**. Choose a category, brand, product type, status, GST registration, business activity, supplier or HSN/SAC and click **Apply**. Every figure and list on the page uses the same filters.
=== step: Act on the gaps | product-dashboard
Each status tile and each line under **Needs attention** opens the product list already filtered — for example all products without an HSN/SAC code — so you can fix them one by one.
=== result
You know how big the catalogue is, what state it is in and which products still need SKUs, tax set-up or suppliers.
=== notes
- All figures come from the database; nothing on the dashboard is sample data.
- The "awaiting approval" link appears when price, GST/HSN or product changes wait in the Approvals inbox.
=== mistakes
- Expecting archived products in the totals of the status filter "Any status" — archived products are counted in their own tile.
