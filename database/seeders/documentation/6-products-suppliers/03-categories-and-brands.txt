summary: Organise products in nested categories and record the brands you buy and sell — without hardcoded lists.
=== what
Categories group products and can be nested: a category can have sub-categories, which can have their own sub-categories (up to ten levels). Brands record the brand name, code, website, manufacturer and an optional logo.
=== who
Categories need **products.manage_categories**; brands need **products.manage_brands**. Everyone with **products.view** can see them.
=== step: Add a category or sub-category | product-categories
Open **Product Management → Categories**. Enter a code and name; choose a **Parent** to make it a sub-category. Add an optional description, display order and image, then save. The list shows the tree with each category's product count.
=== step: Add a brand | product-brands
Open **Product Management → Brands**, enter the code and name, optionally a website (https only), description and logo, and save.
=== result
The categories and brands appear on the product form and as filters on the dashboard, product list and reports.
=== notes
- A category cannot be placed under itself or one of its own sub-categories.
- Archive a category or brand when it is no longer used. Products keep the category or brand they already have; archived ones cannot be chosen for other products.
- A category with active sub-categories cannot be archived until the sub-categories are.
- Category codes are used by the default SKU pattern ({CAT}), so short codes such as HF or LAMP work best.
=== mistakes
- Renaming a category code after many SKUs were generated: existing SKUs do not change, so new SKUs will use the new prefix.
