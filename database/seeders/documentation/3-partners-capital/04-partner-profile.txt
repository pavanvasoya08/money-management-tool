=== what
A partner profile has a **profile card** (photo or initials, partner ID, status, contact, location, date of joining, capital account, linked login, who added it) and **tabs**:

- **Overview**: the financial summary.
- **Personal information**: full details and the login account.
- **Capital contributions** and **Capital withdrawals**: the partner's entries.
- **Capital balance**: the partner's ledger summary.
- **Profit sharing**: the partner's percentage history.
- **Financial reports**: links to partner reports.
- **Documents**: uploaded documents.
- **Activity history**: every change to this partner.

Two further tabs are placeholders for modules that are not released yet and show no data. Each tab appears only if you have permission for its data.
=== why
Everything about a partner is in one place.
=== who
Users with **partners.view** for this partner.
=== step: Open the profile | partner-profile
Click a partner in the **Partners** list.
=== step: Switch tabs
Click a tab name. The address changes, so you can bookmark or share a tab with colleagues who have the same access.
=== step: Use the profile actions
From the card: **Record contribution**, **Change status**, edit, and **Add a profile photo** (JPG, PNG or WEBP).
=== example: Checking a partner before a meeting
Before the partners' meeting, Asha opens Rohan Mehta → **Overview** for his balance (₹2,50,000.00), **Profit sharing** for his 50% share, and **Documents** for the partnership deed.
=== result
You can see the partner's complete picture according to your permissions.
=== notes
- A partner login sees only its own profile.
- Sensitive fields can be hidden or masked by field rules.
=== mistakes
- Expecting the profile to show figures from draft or pending entries as part of the balance. Only posted entries count.
=== faq: Why are some tabs missing for me?
Each tab needs a permission (for example capital ledger view or documents view).
