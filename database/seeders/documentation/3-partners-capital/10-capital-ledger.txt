=== what
The **capital ledger** is a partner's statement: one line per **posted** entry, in date order, with:

- **Date**, **Reference** (entry number), **Particulars**, **Payment ref.**
- **Debit**: money out (withdrawals, reducing adjustments, reversals of contributions)
- **Credit**: money in (contributions, increasing adjustments, reversals of withdrawals)
- **Balance**: the running balance after each line
- **Opening balance** at the start of the chosen period and **Closing balance** at the end.

Drafts, pending, approved-but-unposted, rejected and cancelled entries never appear.
=== why
It is the authoritative history of each partner's capital.
=== who
Users with **capital_ledger.view** for the partner (accountants for assigned partners; partners for their own ledger).
=== step: Open Ledger & Balances | ledger-index
Open **Capital → Ledger & Balances** and click **Ledger** on a partner.
=== step: Choose a period | ledger
Set **From** and **To** and click **Apply**. The opening balance is the balance just before *From*.
=== step: Export
Use **Export → CSV, Excel or PDF**.

| 10 Apr 2026 | CC-2026-00001 | Capital contribution (Bank transfer) | | ₹2,00,000.00 | ₹3,00,000.00 |
| 14 Aug 2026 | CW-2026-00001 | Capital withdrawal | ₹50,000.00 | | ₹2,50,000.00 |
| 02 Sep 2026 | CA-2026-00001 | Correction (reduce) | ₹10,000.00 | | ₹2,40,000.00 |
| 05 Sep 2026 | CR-2026-00001 | Reversal of CA-2026-00001 | | ₹10,000.00 | ₹2,50,000.00 |
| | | **Closing balance** | ₹60,000.00 | ₹3,10,000.00 | **₹2,50,000.00** |

The correction was posted by mistake and reversed, so its net effect is zero.
=== result
You can read each partner's capital position on any date.
=== notes
- **Closing = Opening + Contributions + Adjustments − Withdrawals − Reversed amounts.**
- Amounts are exact to the paisa; there is no rounding.
- Exports contain the same lines and respect hidden or masked amount fields.
=== mistakes
- Comparing the ledger with a total that includes pending entries.
=== faq: Why does an entry I just approved not appear?
It is not posted yet. Only posted entries appear in the ledger.
