summary: Create a partner record and its zero-balance capital account.
=== what
Adding a partner creates the partner record, a unique partner ID and a capital account with a zero balance. Capital is added later through contributions or an opening-capital adjustment.
=== why
Every capital entry must belong to a partner.
=== who
Users with **partners.create**.
=== prerequisites
- The partner's full name and date of joining. PAN and contact details are optional but recommended.
=== step: Open the form | partners-list
In **Partners** click **Add partner**.
=== step: Fill in identity details | partner-create
Enter **Full name** and **Date of joining** (required). Optionally enter **PAN** and a **profile photo**.
=== step: Fill in contact details
Email, mobile number, street address, city, state, country, PIN code and notes.
=== step: Save
Click save. The profile opens with the new partner ID, for example `PTR-0003`, and capital account `CAP-PTR-0003`.
=== example: Adding a partner (illustrative)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Full name | Vikram Singh |
| Date of joining | 15 Jul 2025 |
| PAN | CVSPS9012M |
| Email | vikram@example.com |
| Mobile | 9811122233 |
| City / State | Jaipur / Rajasthan |

Result: partner **PTR-0003** with capital account **CAP-PTR-0003** at ₹0.00.
=== result
A new active partner with a zero-balance capital account.
=== notes
- **PAN** must match the PAN pattern (for example `ABCPE1234F`, with a valid holder-type letter in the 4th position) and must be unique.
- Mobile numbers and PIN codes are validated.
- The record shows who created it and when.
=== mistakes
- Entering a PAN with spaces or lowercase mistakes; type it exactly as on the card.
- Recording capital in the notes field. Use a contribution or an opening-capital adjustment instead.
=== faq: Does adding a partner create a login?
No. Link or create a login from the partner's **Personal information** tab if the partner should sign in.
