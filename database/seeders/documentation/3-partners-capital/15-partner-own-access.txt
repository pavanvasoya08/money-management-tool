summary: A partner's own sign-in shows only their own record and capital.
=== what
When a partner's record is linked to a login with the **Partner** role, the partner sees, read-only:

- their own partner profile and financial summary,
- their own contributions, withdrawals and capital ledger,
- their own documents.

With the default Partner role they do **not** see profit-sharing percentages; a Super Admin can grant Profit Sharing view if partners should see them.

They do not see other partners, configuration screens or administration, and other partners' records answer **Page not found**.
=== why
Partners can check their capital at any time without asking the accounts team.
=== who
Partners with a linked login and the Partner role (as configured by your Super Admin).
=== step: Sign in | login
The partner signs in with the login created for them.
=== step: Open Partners | partner-own-view
The list contains only the partner's own record. Open it to see the summary, ledger and documents.
=== example: Rohan checks his balance
Rohan signs in, opens **Partners → Rohan Mehta → Overview** and sees his current capital balance of ₹2,50,000.00. His dashboard totals also cover only his own capital, not the whole firm's.
=== result
The partner sees their own data only.
=== notes
- What a partner sees is decided by the Partner role's permissions and the **Own records** scope, not by the link itself.
=== mistakes
- Giving the Partner role to a staff member. Staff should use staff roles.
=== faq: Can a partner record a contribution themselves?
Not with the default Partner role, which is view-only. A Super Admin can change that, but recording is normally done by accounts staff.
