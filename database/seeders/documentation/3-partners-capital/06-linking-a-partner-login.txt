summary: Give a partner their own sign-in, linked to their partner record.
=== what
A partner record can be linked to **one** user account, and a user account to **one** partner. From **Personal information → Login account** you can:

- **Link** an existing user,
- **Create login**: a new account with the limited **Partner** role (offered only if you may assign roles),
- **Suspend login** / **Reactivate login**,
- **Unlink**.

Every change is recorded in **Login link history** and the audit log.
=== why
A linked partner can sign in and see their own profile, capital entries, ledger and documents (with the Partner role's “Own records” access).
=== who
Users with **partners.link_account**.
=== step: Open Personal information | partner-login
Open the partner and click **Personal information**. Scroll to **Login account**.
=== step: Link or create a login
Choose an existing user and link it, or create a login with email and a temporary password. The partner must change the password at first sign-in.
=== step: Manage the login
Use **Suspend login**, **Reactivate login** or **Unlink** as needed.
=== example: Giving Rohan access to his own capital
Asha creates a login `rohan.login@example.com` for Rohan Mehta with the Partner role. Rohan signs in and sees only PTR-0001: his ₹2,50,000.00 balance, his ledger and his documents, and nothing about other partners.
=== result
The partner can sign in and sees only their own data, as their permissions allow.
=== notes
- **Linking changes nothing else**: it never adds roles or permissions. What the partner sees comes from the Control Center configuration.
- Unlinking keeps the user account; deactivate it separately if needed.
=== mistakes
- Linking a staff member's account to a partner record by mistake. That user would see the partner's “own” data.
=== faq: Can one person be linked to two partner records?
No. One login, one partner.
