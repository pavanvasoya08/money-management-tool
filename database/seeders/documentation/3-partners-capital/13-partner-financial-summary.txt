=== what
A partner's **Overview** tab shows:

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total contributed** | Posted contributions, net of reversals |
| **Total withdrawn** | Posted withdrawals, net of reversals |
| **Current capital balance** | Closing balance from posted entries only |
| **Opening capital** | Posted opening-capital-migration adjustments |
| **Adjustments (net)** | Other posted adjustments (increases minus reductions) |
| **Pending contributions / withdrawals** | Count and amount awaiting approval or posting (including approved but not posted) |
| **Last contribution / Last withdrawal** | Dates of the latest posted entries |
| **Active profit share** | Today's percentage and its arrangement (shown to users with Profit Sharing view permission) |

Every figure comes from database records at the moment you open the page.
=== why
A single, reliable snapshot of the partner's position.
=== who
Users with **partners.view_financial_summary** for the partner; partners for their own record.
=== step: Open the Overview tab | partner-profile
Open the partner; **Overview** is the first tab.
=== example: Reading Rohan's summary (illustrative)
Total contributed ₹2,00,000 · Total withdrawn ₹50,000 · Opening capital ₹1,00,000 · Adjustments ₹0 → Current capital balance **₹2,50,000** (1,00,000 + 2,00,000 − 50,000). Active profit share 50%.
=== result
You can explain the partner's balance from its parts.
=== notes
- Capital contributions are not revenue and withdrawals are not expenses; they only move the partner's capital account.
- Profit-sharing percentages are configured separately and do not follow capital.
- Hidden or masked amount fields stay hidden or masked here.
=== mistakes
- Adding pending amounts to the balance. They are shown separately for that reason.
=== faq: Why is my balance different from the dashboard total?
The dashboard totals all partners you may see; the summary covers one partner.
