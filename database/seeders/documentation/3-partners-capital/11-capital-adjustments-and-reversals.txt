summary: Adjust capital with a reason, and correct posted entries by reversal.
=== what
**Capital adjustments** change a partner's capital without a contribution or withdrawal. Each has:

- a **type**: *Opening capital migration*, *Correction of historical balance*, *Approved capital reclassification* or *Other*,
- an **effect**: **increase** or **reduce** capital,
- a mandatory **reason** and an optional supporting document.

Adjustments are numbered `CA-…`, go through the **Capital adjustment approval** workflow, and change the balance only when posted.

**Reversals** correct mistakes in posted entries. Posted entries are **never edited or deleted**. Instead, **Reverse entry** creates a new posted entry (`CR-…`) for the same amount in the opposite direction. The two entries are linked both ways and the original becomes **Reversed**. You then record a correct replacement if needed.
=== why
Corrections must be transparent: anyone reading the ledger can see the original, the reversal and the replacement.
=== who
Adjustments: **capital_adjustments.create / approve / post**. Reversals: the **.reverse** permission of the entry's module.
=== step: Record an adjustment | adjustment-form
Open **Capital → Adjustments → New**. Choose the partner, date, amount, **Adjustment type**, **Effect** and **Reason**, and attach support. Submit, then post after approval.
=== step: Reverse a posted entry | contribution-show
Open the posted entry. Under **Reverse this posted entry**, enter the **reason** and the **reversal date**, click **Reverse entry** and confirm.
=== step: Record the replacement
Create a new entry with the correct details and take it through approval and posting.
=== example: Correcting a wrong amount (illustrative)
A contribution of ₹1,50,000 was posted as ₹15,000 (`CC-2026-00003`).

1. Reverse `CC-2026-00003` with reason “Amount keyed wrongly; bank advice shows ₹1,50,000” → `CR-2026-00001` debits ₹15,000. Net effect of the pair: zero.
2. Record and post a new contribution of ₹1,50,000 (`CC-2026-00004`).

The ledger shows all three lines; nothing was deleted.
=== result
Balances are corrected and the full history stays visible.
=== notes
- An entry can be reversed only once, and posting is protected against duplicates.
- Reversing a contribution whose money has already been withdrawn is blocked if it would take the balance below zero (unless the exception policy applies).
- Every adjustment and reversal is in the audit log with actor, IP, and old and new values.
=== mistakes
- Using an *Other* adjustment to hide a correction. Choose the right type and explain it in the reason.
- Reversing without recording the correct replacement.
=== faq: Can I reverse a reversal?
No. Record a new entry instead.
