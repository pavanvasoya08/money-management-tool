summary: How each partner's capital account works and how to bring in an opening balance.
=== what
Each partner has one **capital account** (for example `CAP-PTR-0001`). Its balance changes only through **posted** entries:

- **Contributions** increase capital.
- **Withdrawals** reduce capital.
- **Adjustments** increase or reduce capital (for example an opening balance or a correction).
- **Reversals** cancel a posted entry by posting the opposite amount.

**Opening balance:** if the business already existed before the ERP, record each partner's capital brought forward as a **Capital adjustment** of type **Opening capital migration** with effect **Increase capital**. On the partner's financial summary it appears as **Opening capital**.

**Capital summary:** **Capital → Ledger & Balances** shows every partner's opening capital, contributions, adjustments, withdrawals, reversed amounts, closing capital and pending amounts, as of any date.
=== why
Starting with correct opening balances makes every later balance and profit-sharing discussion reliable.
=== who
Opening balances are recorded by users with **capital_adjustments.create**, approved by approvers, and posted by users with **capital_adjustments.post**.
=== step: Record the opening balance | adjustment-form
Open **Capital → Adjustments → New**. Choose the partner, the date (for example the first day in the ERP), the amount, type **Opening capital migration**, effect **Increase capital**, and a reason such as “Capital as per books on 31 Mar 2025”. Attach the closing balance sheet if available.
=== step: Submit, approve and post
Submit for approval. After approval, click **Post to ledger**.
=== step: Review all partners' balances | ledger-index
Open **Capital → Ledger & Balances**. Choose an **As of** date to see balances on that day.
=== example: Migrating from old books (illustrative)
Rohan Mehta's capital in the old books on 31 Mar 2025 was ₹1,00,000. An adjustment `CA-2025-00001` (Opening capital migration, increase, ₹1,00,000, dated 1 Apr 2025) is approved and posted. Rohan's summary now shows **Opening capital ₹1,00,000.00**.
=== result
Each partner's capital account starts from the correct figure.
=== notes
- Capital is **not** income: opening balances and contributions never appear as revenue.
- The balance formula is: **Closing = Opening + Contributions + Adjustments − Withdrawals − Reversed amounts**, using posted entries only.
- All amounts are stored exactly in rupees and paise; nothing is rounded.
=== mistakes
- Recording an opening balance as a contribution. It would look like new money received in the current period.
- Forgetting to post: approved but unposted entries do not change balances.
=== faq: Can I edit an opening balance after posting?
No. Posted entries are never edited. Reverse it and record the correct one. See **Capital adjustments, corrections & reversals**.
