=== what
**Partner Reports** offers eight reports:

1. Partner list
2. Capital contributions
3. Capital withdrawals
4. Capital balances (with an **as-of** date)
5. Partner-wise capital ledger
6. Profit-sharing configuration
7. Pending contributions
8. Pending withdrawals

Reports have date, partner and status filters, search and pagination, and can be exported as **CSV**, **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF**, or printed.
=== why
For partner meetings, reconciliations, auditors and the partners themselves.
=== who
Viewing a report needs **partner_reports.view** plus view access to the report's source module. Exporting needs **partner_reports.export** plus that module's export permission.
=== step: Open Partner Reports | partner-reports
Click **Partner Reports** in the sidebar and choose a report.
=== step: Filter
Set dates, partner and status, then apply.
=== step: Export or print | report-balances
Choose **CSV**, **Excel** or **PDF**, or **Print**.
=== example: Capital balances at year end (illustrative)
Open **Capital balances**, set **As of** 31 Mar 2026, and export to Excel for the auditor. Each row shows opening capital, contributions, adjustments, withdrawals, reversed amounts, closing capital and pending amounts.
=== result
A report and export containing exactly what you are allowed to see.
=== notes
- The same record scope and field rules apply on screen and in all three export formats: hidden columns are removed and masked values stay masked.
- Exports are recorded in the activity log.
=== mistakes
- Sharing an exported file with someone who should not see it. The ERP controls access inside the system, not after download.
=== faq: Why does my export contain fewer partners than my colleague's?
Exports follow your data visibility.
