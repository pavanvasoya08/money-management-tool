=== what
From a partner's profile you can **edit** the details and change the **status**:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Deactivate / Activate** | Marks the partner inactive or active again |
| **Suspend access** | Suspends the partner with a reason. If you may suspend users, the linked login is suspended too |
| **Reactivate** | Ends a suspension |
| **Archive** | Closes the partner. Requires a **zero posted capital balance** and **no open entries**. The profile becomes read-only and history is kept |
| **Restore** | Brings an archived partner back |
=== why
Partners join, pause and leave; their records must stay accurate without losing history.
=== who
Users with **partners.edit** (details), **partners.change_status**, **partners.suspend** and **partners.archive**.
=== step: Open the partner's profile | partner-profile
Click the partner in the **Partners** list.
=== step: Edit details
Click the edit (pencil) button on the profile card, change the fields and save. Changes are audited, with PAN masked in the audit log.
=== step: Change status
Click **Change status** and choose the action. Enter a reason where asked and confirm.
=== example: A partner retires
Vikram retires. First his remaining capital is paid out through a posted withdrawal, so his balance is ₹0.00 and he has no open entries. Then **Change status → Archive partner**. His profile and ledger remain available, read-only.
=== result
The partner's status and details are updated, with a full audit trail.
=== notes
- Archived partners cannot receive new entries or documents.
- Status changes do not delete any capital entry.
=== mistakes
- Trying to archive a partner who still has capital or a pending entry; the ERP blocks it.
=== faq: Can I delete a partner?
No. Partners are archived, never deleted, so the capital history stays complete.
