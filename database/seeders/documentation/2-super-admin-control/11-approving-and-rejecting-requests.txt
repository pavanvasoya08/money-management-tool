summary: Use the Approvals inbox to approve or reject entries waiting for you.
=== what
The **Approvals** inbox lists requests **Waiting for you** (you can decide them now) and requests **Submitted by you**. Each request shows the workflow, amount, requester, status and current step. The bell in the top bar shows a red dot when something is waiting.
=== why
Entries such as withdrawals do not take effect until the required approvers agree.
=== who
Users who can approve or reject in at least one module (the **Approvals** menu item appears for them).
=== step: Open the inbox | approvals-inbox
Click **Approvals** in the sidebar, or the bell, then **View all approvals**.
=== step: Open a request | approval-decide
Click the request to see its details, its steps and its history.
=== step: Approve or reject
To approve, optionally add a comment and click **Approve**. To reject, type the **Rejection reason** (required) and click **Reject**.
=== step: Cancel your own request (requesters)
If you submitted a request by mistake, open it and click **Cancel my request**.
=== example: Approving a withdrawal
Neha submits a ₹25,000 withdrawal for Priya Nair. Asha sees it under **Waiting for you**, checks that Priya's eligible capital covers it, and clicks **Approve**. The withdrawal becomes **Approved**; it reduces Priya's balance only when someone **posts** it.
=== result
The request moves to the next step, or becomes Approved or Rejected. The linked entry's status updates automatically.
=== notes
- You cannot approve your own request (unless an administrator turned that protection off).
- A rejected capital entry can be corrected and resubmitted: editing it returns it to Draft.
=== mistakes
- Approving without opening the entry and its supporting document.
=== faq: Why can I see a request but not decide it?
It is waiting at a step assigned to someone else, or you submitted it yourself.
