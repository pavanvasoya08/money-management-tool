summary: Hide, mask or lock individual fields such as PAN, phone numbers or amounts.
=== what
Inside a record you can control each **field**. The levels, from least to most access:

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hidden** | Not shown anywhere: page, export or API |
| **Masked** | Partly shown, e.g. PAN `XXXXXX234K`, email `r****@example.com`, mobile `******2340`, amount `₹ ••••••`. Never editable |
| **Visible** | Shown in lists and detail pages, not editable |
| **Read-only** | Shown everywhere, including forms (locked), not editable |
| **Editable** | Shown and can be changed (the module's Edit permission is still required) |

Each field has a **default** level. A **role rule** can change it (if a user has several roles, the most permissive role wins), and a **user rule** overrides everything for that user.
=== why
Some people need a record but not its sensitive details: a manager may need the partner directory but not PAN numbers or capital amounts.
=== who
Super Admins configure; it applies to every user.
=== step: Set field rules per role | field-visibility
Open **Control Center → Field visibility**, choose the module, set the **Default** and each role's level, and click **Save field visibility**.
=== step: Override for one user (optional) | cc-user-fields
In **Control Center → User management → Field permissions**, set a **Rule** for a field. The **Effective** column shows the result. Click **Save field overrides**.
=== example: Protecting PAN and capital figures (illustrative)
| Field | Default | Manager | Accountant |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAN | Masked | Masked | Visible |
| Capital balance | Visible | Hidden | Visible |

Karan (Manager) sees PAN as `XXXXXX234K` and no capital balance; in his CSV export the capital column is removed and PAN stays masked. Neha (Accountant) sees both.
=== result
Sensitive data is hidden or masked consistently on pages, exports and the API.
=== notes
- Rules are enforced on the server. A crafted form that tries to change a read-only field is rejected and logged.
- Identity documents (PAN and ID uploads) follow the **PAN & identity documents** field rule.
=== mistakes
- Hiding a field that a form requires for the user's job, which stops them from saving.
=== faq: Which wins: role or user rule?
The user rule always wins. Without a user rule, the most permissive of the user's roles wins.
