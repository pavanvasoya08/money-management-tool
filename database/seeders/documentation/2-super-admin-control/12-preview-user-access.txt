summary: See exactly what any user can open, do and see — without signing in as them.
=== what
**User access preview** computes a user's real access from their roles, overrides, scopes and field rules, and shows:

- the **sidebar** they would see (✓ or ✗ per item),
- every **page** with the permission it requires and whether it is **Allowed** or **Blocked**,
- each module's **actions**, **data scope** and **field access**, with the reason for each decision.

**Preview is not impersonation.** You stay signed in as yourself, nothing is performed as the user, and the preview is read-only. Each preview is recorded in the audit log.
=== why
To check a configuration before telling someone “you now have access”, or to answer “why can't I see this?”.
=== who
Super Admins.
=== step: Open User access preview | preview
In the Control Center click **User access preview**, or **Preview access** on a user's Control Center profile.
=== step: Choose the user
Select the user. The banner confirms **PREVIEW MODE — read-only**.
=== step: Read the results
Check the sidebar, the pages list and the module details. Each blocked item explains why.
=== example: Checking a new accountant
After assigning Priya and Vikram to Neha, Asha previews Neha: Partners ✓, Capital Contributions ✓, Capital Adjustments ✗, Profit Sharing ✗, Control Center ✗. That matches the Accountant role, so Asha tells Neha she can start.
=== result
You know precisely what the user will experience.
=== notes
- The preview reflects access at this moment, including expiring overrides and account restrictions.
=== mistakes
- Asking a user for their password to “see what they see”. Never do this; use preview.
=== faq: Can I click through pages as that user?
No. Preview shows what they could access; it does not let you act as them.
