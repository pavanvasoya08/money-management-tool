=== what
The **permission matrix** is a grid: one row per module, one column per action. A ticked box means the role grants that permission. Tools above it:

- **Copy permissions from another role**.
- **Search** and a module filter.
- **Select all** / **Deselect all**; clicking a column header toggles the whole column, and the row checkbox toggles the whole row.
- **Effective preview**: how many permissions across how many modules.
- An unsaved-changes counter, and a warning if you leave the page with unsaved changes.
=== why
It is the fastest way to see and set everything a role can do.
=== who
Super Admins.
=== step: Open a role's matrix | matrix
In **Role management** click **Matrix** on the role (or open the role's **Permissions** tab).
=== step: Tick and untick
Tick the actions the role should have. Dashes mean the action does not exist for that module.
=== step: Confirm high-impact actions
When you tick actions such as delete, approve or export, confirm the prompt.
=== step: Save
Click **Save permissions**. The change applies to every user with the role on their next page load, and is audited with the old and new lists.
=== example: Copying and trimming
To create a read-only auditor role: create the role, use **Copy permissions from another role → Accountant**, then untick every Create, Edit, Approve and Post column. Save.
=== result
The role grants exactly the ticked permissions.
=== notes
- How a permission combines with user overrides, data visibility and field rules is explained in **User-specific permission overrides**, **Record-level permissions** and **Field-level permissions**.
=== mistakes
- Forgetting to click **Save permissions**. The unsaved-changes counter reminds you.
=== faq: Do changes affect users who are signed in?
Yes, on their next request.
