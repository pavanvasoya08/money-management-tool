=== what
**Login sessions** lists every active session: user, start time, last activity, IP address and device. It also shows **recent failed sign-ins**. You can **revoke** one session, or end all sessions of a user with **Force logout** on their Control Center profile.
=== why
To respond quickly to a lost laptop, a leaver, or suspicious activity.
=== who
Super Admins.
=== step: Open Login sessions | sessions
In the Control Center click **Login sessions**.
=== step: Revoke a session
Click **Revoke** on the session. That browser is signed out on its next request.
=== step: End all sessions of a user
Open the user in **Control Center → User management** and click **Force logout**.
=== example: A lost phone
Vikram reports his phone lost at 20:15. Asha finds his session from “Android · Chrome” and clicks **Revoke**, then asks him to change his password from his laptop.
=== result
The revoked session can no longer be used; any further click on it leads to the sign-in page.
=== notes
- Revoking ends the session but does not block the account. To block it, deactivate or suspend the user.
- Every revoke and force logout is audited.
=== mistakes
- Revoking a session but forgetting to change a password that may be compromised.
=== faq: Will the user lose unsaved work?
Yes, anything not yet saved in that browser is lost.
