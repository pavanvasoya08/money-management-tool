summary: Reusable permission sets you can apply to roles or individual users.
=== what
A **permission template** is a saved set of permissions, optionally with data scopes. You can:

- **Apply to a role**: the role's permissions are **replaced** by the template's (you are asked to confirm).
- **Apply to a user**: permissions the user is missing are **added** as explicit Allow overrides.
- **Create**, **edit**, **duplicate** and **delete** templates.

Six starter templates are provided. Review them before use; permissions for modules that are not released yet have no effect.
=== why
Templates make it quick and consistent to set up common job profiles.
=== who
Super Admins.
=== step: Open Permission templates | templates
In the Control Center click **Permission templates**.
=== step: Create or edit
Click **Create template** (or **Edit / apply**), name it, tick permissions in the matrix, optionally set data scopes, and save.
=== step: Apply
At the top of the template, choose a role or a user and click **Apply**. Confirm.
=== example: Setting up a read-only role
Create a role *Auditor*, then apply the starter template **Read-Only Auditor** to it. The role's permissions become exactly the template's: view and export, no changes.
=== result
The role or user receives the template's permissions; the change is audited.
=== notes
- Applying to a role replaces its permissions, so apply to an empty or copied role if you want to keep the original.
- Deleting a template does not change roles or users it was applied to.
- Super-Admin-only permissions are never granted by templates.
=== mistakes
- Applying a template to a system role and losing its existing permissions.
=== faq: Can I undo applying a template to a role?
The audit log records the role's previous permissions; re-tick them in the matrix or apply another template.
