summary: Create custom roles, edit, duplicate, enable/disable or delete them safely.
=== what
**Control Center → Role management** shows every role as a card with its type, description, number of users and permissions. From a role you can open its **Permissions** matrix, **Data visibility**, **Field access**, **Users**, **Change history** and **Details**.

You can create unlimited **custom roles**, edit them, **duplicate** any role, **enable or disable** a role (a disabled role grants nothing) and **delete** custom roles.
=== why
Custom roles fit your business exactly, for example *Senior Accountant* or *Branch Manager*, without giving people more than they need.
=== who
Super Admins.
=== step: Open Role management | roles
In the Control Center menu click **Role management**.
=== step: Create a role | role-create
Click **Create role**, enter a name and description, and save. The new role has no permissions yet.
=== step: Grant permissions
Open the role's **Permissions** tab (the matrix) and tick what it may do. See **The permission matrix**.
=== step: Edit, duplicate or disable
On the role's **Details** tab, change name or description, untick **Role enabled** to disable it, or enter a new name under **Duplicate role** to copy it with all its permissions.
=== step: Delete a custom role
Delete is offered for custom roles only. If users hold the role you must reassign them to another active role first.
=== example: A “Senior Accountant” role
Duplicate **Accountant** as *Senior Accountant*, then in its matrix tick **Capital Contributions → Post to capital ledger** and **Approve**. Assign it to Neha. Other accountants keep the original role.
=== result
Roles match the jobs in your business.
=== notes
- System roles (Super Admin, Partner, Accountant, Manager, Viewer) cannot be deleted, but you can change their permissions or disable them.
- A role used as an approver in an approval workflow cannot be deleted until the workflow is changed.
- Users are never moved in bulk to Super Admin during deletion.
- Every change is recorded with a full snapshot in the audit log.
=== mistakes
- Disabling a role without checking who uses it; those users lose its access immediately.
=== faq: If a user has two roles, which wins?
Permissions are combined: anything either role allows is allowed, unless a user-specific Deny override blocks it.
