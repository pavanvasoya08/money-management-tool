=== what
The **Control Center** is the administration area for Super Admins. Its left menu has 14 sections: Overview, User management, Role management, Module permissions, Action permissions, Data visibility, Field visibility, Resource assignments, Approval workflows, Permission templates, User access preview, Login sessions, Audit logs and Security settings.

The **Overview** page shows:

- **User statistics**: registered, active, inactive and suspended users.
- **Permission information**: total and custom roles, active permission assignments, users with restricted access (overrides or restrictions), and pending access requests.
- **Activity**: active sessions, pending approvals and active workflows, recent permission changes and recent administrative activity.
- **Quick actions**: Create user, Create role, Manage permissions, Manage data visibility, Manage field access, Manage approval workflows, View audit logs, Preview user access.
=== why
It is the single place to decide and review access. Every change applies immediately and is audited with old and new values.
=== who
Only real Super Admins. Control Center permissions cannot be granted to any other role, override or template, so nobody can raise their own access.
=== prerequisites
- The Super Admin role.
=== step: Open the Control Center | cc-overview
Click **Control Center** in the sidebar (or in your profile menu).
=== step: Review the numbers
Click a tile to open the related page, for example **Active sessions → Manage sessions**.
=== step: Use the left menu
Each section is explained in its own guide in this category.
=== example: A monthly access review
On the first working day of each month, Asha opens the Overview: 1 user with restricted access, 1 pending approval, no suspended users. She opens **User access preview** for each new joiner and **Audit logs** filtered to the last month.
=== result
You can see the state of access across the business at a glance.
=== notes
- Super Admins cannot change their own access (overrides, suspension, restrictions) from the Control Center. Another Super Admin must do it.
- Hiding a menu item never replaces these server-side rules.
=== mistakes
- Making several Super Admins “for convenience”. Keep the number small and use roles for everyday work.
=== faq: A user has “Control Center” permission in a template. Will they get in?
No. Control Center permissions are Super-Admin-only and are ignored everywhere else.
