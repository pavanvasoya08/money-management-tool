=== what
The ERP is a web application for running the finances of a partnership business. Three areas are live today:

- **Setup & authentication**: secure sign-in, password rules, sessions, user accounts, roles and an activity log.
- **Super Admin Control Center**: fine-grained control over who can open each module, perform each action, see each record and see or edit each field, plus approval workflows.
- **Partners & capital**: partner profiles, capital contributions, withdrawals, adjustments, the capital ledger, profit-sharing percentages and partner reports.

Modules shown in the sidebar under **Upcoming modules** (GST, banking, operations, accounting) are disabled and marked “Soon”. They contain no data yet.
=== why
One system replaces scattered spreadsheets: every partner's capital is recorded entry by entry, every change is approved and audited, and each person sees only what they are allowed to see.
=== who
Everyone in the business who has an account: Super Admins, partners, accountants, managers and read-only viewers. What each person can do depends on the roles and permissions an administrator gives them.
=== prerequisites
- An account created by an administrator. There is no public sign-up.
- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
=== step: Sign in | login
Open the ERP address your administrator gave you and sign in with your email and password. If this is your first sign-in you will be asked to choose a new password.
=== step: Look around the layout | layout
The sidebar on the left lists the modules you may open. The top bar holds search, notifications and your profile menu. See **Application layout & navigation**.
=== step: Check your dashboard
The dashboard shows only figures you are allowed to see. A partner sees their own capital; an administrator sees firm-wide totals.
=== step: Open this guide whenever you need it
**Help & User Guide** is at the bottom of the sidebar and is available to every user. Use the search box to find any task.
=== example: Your first day as an accountant
Neha joins as an accountant. The administrator creates her account with the Accountant role and assigns her two partners.

1. Neha signs in with the temporary password and sets her own password.
2. Her sidebar shows Partners, Capital and Partner Reports, but not the Control Center.
3. In Partners she sees only the two partners assigned to her.
4. She opens **Help & User Guide → Partners & Capital** to learn how to record a contribution.
=== result
You know which areas exist, where to find them, and why you may see fewer menu items than a colleague.
=== notes
- Hiding a menu item is only cosmetic. Every page and action is also checked on the server, so typing a web address you are not allowed to open shows **Access denied**.
- The help guide is read-only for everyone. Only users with documentation permissions can change it.
=== mistakes
- Assuming a missing menu item is a bug. Usually it means you do not have that permission; ask your administrator.
- Sharing one account between several people. Every action is recorded against the signed-in person.
=== faq: Why do I see fewer modules than my colleague?
Each user's sidebar is built from their own roles and permissions. Ask your administrator if you need access to something.
=== faq: Are the “Soon” modules usable?
No. They are placeholders for later releases and contain no data or figures.
