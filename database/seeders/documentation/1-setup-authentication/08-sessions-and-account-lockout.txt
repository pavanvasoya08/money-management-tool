summary: Why you may be signed out or temporarily locked, and what to do.
=== what
A **session** is your signed-in visit. The ERP protects it with several rules:

- **Inactivity timeout**: you are signed out after a period without activity (30 minutes by default).
- **Absolute limit**: every session ends after a maximum time (8 hours by default), even if you stay active.
- **Device check**: a session cannot be moved to another browser.
- **Automatic sign-out** when your account is deactivated, suspended or expired, when your password is changed, or when an administrator ends your sessions.
- An administrator may limit how many sessions a user can have at the same time.

**Account lockout**: after several wrong passwords in a row (5 by default), your account is locked for a set time (15 minutes by default). There is also a limit per network: too many failed attempts from one IP address (20 within 15 minutes) blocks further attempts from it for a while.
=== why
These rules stop someone from guessing passwords, using a stolen session or staying signed in on an unattended computer.
=== who
Every user. Super Admins can adjust the numbers in **Control Center → Security settings**.
=== step: If you were signed out
Sign in again. Unsaved form input may be lost, so save drafts regularly when entering long records.
=== step: If your account is locked
Wait for the lockout time to pass, then sign in with the correct password. If you have forgotten it, use **Forgot password**.
=== step: If you see “Too many failed sign-in attempts from your network”
Wait 15 minutes. This limit covers everyone signing in from the same network.
=== example: Lockout timeline (default settings)
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 10:00–10:02 | 5 wrong passwords |
| 10:02 | Account locked for 15 minutes; even the correct password is refused |
| 10:17 | Lock expires; correct password signs in |
=== result
You understand why sign-in was refused or why you were signed out, and how to continue.
=== notes
- Locked accounts appear as **Locked now** on administrators' dashboards. Every failed attempt and lockout is recorded.
- The error message for a wrong password never says whether the email exists.
=== mistakes
- Repeatedly guessing a password, which extends the problem. Use **Forgot password** instead.
=== faq: Can an administrator unlock me immediately?
The lock expires automatically. An administrator can also set a new temporary password for you.
