summary: What each dashboard card means and why different users see different figures.
=== what
The dashboard is the first page after sign-in. It is made of cards, and each card appears only if you have permission for its data:

- **Your account**: your name, roles, last sign-in time and IP address, and your access level.
- **Overview (Users & access)**: total, active, inactive and currently locked users. Click a tile to open the filtered user list.
- **Quick actions**: shortcuts to pages you may open.
- **Partners & capital**: total and active partners, pending contributions and withdrawals, total posted capital, total posted withdrawals and total outstanding capital.
- **Partner-wise capital distribution**: a bar chart of each partner's closing capital, with a **Show as table** option.
- **Sign-in activity**, **Recent login activity** and **Recent system activity** for users who may view them.
=== why
It gives a quick, trustworthy picture of the business and of your own pending work.
=== who
Every user. Super Admins and accountants see more cards; a partner sees only their own capital; a viewer may see only the account card.
=== step: Read the cards | dashboard
Each figure is calculated from real records at the moment the page loads. Capital figures use **posted** entries only; drafts and entries awaiting approval are shown separately as *pending*.
=== step: Drill down
Click a tile, for example **Pending withdrawals**, to open the matching filtered list.
=== example: Reading the capital cards (illustrative figures)
| Card | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Total posted capital | ₹6,50,000.00 | Contributions posted, net of reversals |
| Total posted withdrawals | ₹50,000.00 | Withdrawals posted; not a business expense |
| Total outstanding capital | ₹7,00,000.00 | Capital still in the business, including opening balances and adjustments |
| Pending withdrawals | 1 · ₹25,000.00 | Submitted but not yet approved or posted; does not change balances yet |
=== result
You can explain every number on your dashboard and where it comes from.
=== notes
- Capital contributions are not revenue and withdrawals are not expenses. The dashboard does not show profit, because profit calculation is not part of the current release.
- If you can see only some partners, every total covers only those partners.
=== mistakes
- Expecting a newly recorded contribution to change totals immediately. It changes them only after approval **and** posting.
=== faq: Why does my dashboard look different from my manager's?
Cards and figures depend on each user's permissions and record access.
=== faq: The chart says “Show as table”. Why?
The table is an accessible alternative and also appears if the chart library cannot load.
