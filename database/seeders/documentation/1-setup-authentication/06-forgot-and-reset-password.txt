summary: Request a secure reset link by email and choose a new password.
=== what
If you cannot remember your password, you can request a one-time reset link. The link is sent to your account email, is valid for a limited time (60 minutes by default) and works only once.
=== why
It lets you regain access without an administrator ever knowing your password.
=== who
Any user who has forgotten their password.
=== prerequisites
- Access to the mailbox of your account email.
=== step: Open “Forgot password” | forgot-password
On the sign-in page click **Forgot password**.
=== step: Enter your email and send the link
Type your account email and submit. The ERP always answers: **“If an active account exists for that email, a password reset link has been sent.”** This protects accounts from being discovered by strangers.
=== step: Open the link in the email
Click the link in the email within the validity time. A page to **set a new password** opens.
=== step: Choose a new password
Enter and confirm a password that meets the policy (at least 10 characters by default, with uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol, and not containing your name or email). Then sign in with it.
=== example: Resetting before a month-end close
Neha forgets her password on the last day of the month. She requests a link at 9:05, receives the email, and sets a new password at 9:07. The link cannot be used again afterwards.
=== result
Your password is changed and you can sign in with the new one.
=== notes
- Requesting a new link makes any earlier link invalid.
- Only a limited number of reset requests are accepted per hour (3 by default) to prevent abuse.
- If the page says **Link expired or invalid**, request a fresh link.
- Where the email is delivered depends on how your administrator configured mail. In a local test setup, messages may be written to the server's mail log instead of being sent.
=== mistakes
- Using an old link after requesting a newer one.
- Waiting longer than the validity period.
=== faq: I did not receive the email.
Check spam, confirm you used your account email, and wait a few minutes. If it still does not arrive, ask an administrator to set a temporary password for you from User Management.
=== faq: Can an administrator see my new password?
No. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
