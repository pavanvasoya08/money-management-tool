summary: Update a user's details, roles or password from their profile.
=== what
From a user's profile you can:

- **Edit details**: name, email and phone.
- **Change roles**: tick or untick roles and click **Save roles**.
- **Reset password**: set a temporary password the user must change at next sign-in.

The profile also shows login history and every change made to the account.
=== why
People change jobs, names and phone numbers; access must follow.
=== who
Users with **users.edit** (details), **users.assign_roles** (roles) and **users.reset_password** (passwords).
=== step: Open the user's profile | user-profile
In **User Management** click **View** on the user.
=== step: Edit details
Click **Edit details**, change the fields and save.
=== step: Change roles
In the **Roles** card tick the new roles, untick the old ones and click **Save roles**. The change applies on the user's next page load.
=== step: Reset the password (if needed)
In **Reset password**, enter and confirm a temporary password, then click **Set temporary password**. The user is signed out of remembered devices and must choose a new password at next sign-in.
=== example: Promoting a viewer to manager
Meera moves from a read-only role to managing operations. The administrator unticks **Viewer**, ticks **Manager** and saves. Meera's sidebar now includes the modules the Manager role allows.
=== result
The account reflects the person's current details and job.
=== notes
- You cannot change your own roles or status.
- Only Super Admins can grant or remove the Super Admin role or manage Super Admin accounts.
- The last active Super Admin cannot lose the Super Admin role.
- Every change is written to the activity log with the old and new values.
=== mistakes
- Removing a role before giving the replacement, leaving the user without access in between.
=== faq: Where do I set per-user exceptions such as “can view but not export”?
In **Control Center → User management → Module permissions** (Super Admins only).
