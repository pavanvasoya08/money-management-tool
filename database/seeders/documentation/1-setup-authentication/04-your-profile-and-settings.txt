=== what
**Settings → Profile** (open it from your profile menu) shows:

- **Personal information**: name, email, roles, phone, last sign-in, when your password was last changed, and when your account was created.
- **My permissions**: what your roles allow. Super Admins see “unrestricted access”.
- **My recent sign-ins**: time, result, IP address and device of your latest sign-in attempts.

The left menu also links to **Security** (change password) and, if you may view it, the **Activity log**.
=== why
To confirm what you can do, and to spot sign-ins you do not recognise.
=== who
Every user, for their own account.
=== step: Open your profile | account-profile
Click your initials in the top bar and choose **Profile**.
=== step: Check your sign-ins
Look at **My recent sign-ins**. If you see a sign-in you do not recognise, change your password immediately and tell your administrator.
=== example: Spotting an unknown sign-in
Priya notices a failed sign-in from an IP address she does not know. She changes her password and informs the Super Admin, who checks **Control Center → Login sessions**.
=== result
You know your access and can verify that only you use your account.
=== notes
- You cannot change your own roles or email here. Ask an administrator.
=== mistakes
- Ignoring failed sign-ins you did not make.
=== faq: Can I change my name or email myself?
No. An administrator with user-edit permission changes these from User Management.
