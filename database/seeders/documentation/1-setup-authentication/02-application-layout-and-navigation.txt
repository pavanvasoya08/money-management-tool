=== what
Every page shares the same layout:

- **Sidebar (left)**: modules grouped into sections (Overview, Partners & Capital, Upcoming modules, Administration, Help & Support). Items with an arrow, such as **Capital**, open a sub-menu.
- **Top bar**: sidebar toggle, quick navigation search, notifications, settings (if you may view settings) and your profile menu.
- **Breadcrumbs**: the path to the current page, for example *Home / Partners / Rohan Mehta*. Click any part to go back.
- **Footer**: copyright and the application version.
=== why
A consistent layout means you always know where to find a feature, and the quick search takes you to any permitted page in two keystrokes.
=== who
Every user.
=== step: Use the sidebar | layout
Click a module to open it. Click a group such as **Capital** to show Contributions, Withdrawals, Adjustments and Ledger & Balances. The current page is highlighted in blue.
=== step: Collapse or expand the sidebar
Click the menu button at the top-left of the top bar. On a desktop the sidebar shrinks to icons and the ERP remembers your choice. On a phone the button opens and closes the sidebar.
=== step: Jump anywhere with quick navigation
Click the search icon, or press **Ctrl + K** (**⌘ + K** on a Mac) or **/**. Type part of a page name and press **Enter**. Only pages you may open are listed.
=== step: Check notifications
The bell shows a red dot when something waits for you, such as an approval request. Click it to see the items and **View all approvals**.
=== step: Open your profile menu | profile-menu
Click your initials at the top-right for **Profile**, **Security** (change password), **Activity log** (if permitted), **Control Center** (Super Admins only) and **Sign out**.
=== example: Finding the capital ledger quickly
Instead of opening Capital → Ledger & Balances, press **Ctrl + K**, type `ledger` and press **Enter**.
=== result
You can move around the ERP confidently using the sidebar, breadcrumbs or quick search.
=== notes
- The menu only shows what you may open, but the server checks every request again. A hidden page is not just hidden, it is blocked.
- Disabled items marked **Soon** belong to later releases.
=== mistakes
- Using the browser's back button after signing out. Signed-out pages are not cached, so you will be asked to sign in again.
=== faq: The sidebar disappeared on my phone. How do I get it back?
Tap the menu button at the top-left. Tap outside the sidebar to close it again.
=== faq: Why is the settings (gear) icon missing for me?
It is shown only to users with the Settings view permission.
