summary: The five built-in roles and what they are generally for.
=== what
A **role** is a named set of permissions. Five roles come with the ERP:

| Role | General purpose |
|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Unrestricted access to everything, including the Control Center. Can only be granted by another Super Admin. |
| **Accountant** | Accounting staff. By default works on **assigned** partners: views them, records contributions and withdrawals as drafts and submits them, and views and exports their ledgers and reports. Cannot change partner profiles or post entries by default. |
| **Manager** | Operations manager. By default reads users and activity and sees the partner directory with PAN masked, but no capital figures and no profit sharing. |
| **Partner** | A business partner's own login. Sees only **their own** partner profile, financial summary, documents, contributions, withdrawals and ledger, read-only. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to the dashboard. |
=== why
Roles make access consistent: give a person the role that matches their job instead of picking permissions one by one.
=== who
Administrators assign roles; everyone works within them.
=== step: See a role's permissions
Super Admins open **Control Center → Role management** and click **Matrix** on a role.
=== step: Assign roles to a user
In **User Management → View user → Roles**, tick the roles and click **Save roles**.
=== example: Same module, different roles
Neha (Accountant, assigned to Priya and Vikram) and Rohan (Partner login) both open **Partners**. Neha sees Priya Nair and Vikram Singh; Rohan sees only his own record, Rohan Mehta.
=== result
You know what each built-in role is meant for.
=== notes
- The table above describes the **defaults**. Actual access depends on how your Super Admin configured each role, plus any user-specific overrides, data visibility and field rules. Always check **Settings → Profile → My permissions** for your own access.
- A user can hold several roles; they get the combined permissions (unless a Deny override blocks something).
- Custom roles can be created in the Control Center.
=== mistakes
- Assuming the role name alone tells you what someone can do. The configuration decides.
=== faq: Can a role be renamed or deleted?
Custom roles can be renamed or deleted. System roles cannot be deleted, but their permissions can be changed or the role disabled.
