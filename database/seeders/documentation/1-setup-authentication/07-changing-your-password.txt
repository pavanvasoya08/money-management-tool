summary: Change your password from Settings → Security.
=== what
You can change your password at any time. You must know your current password. If an administrator gave you a temporary password, the ERP makes you change it at your first sign-in.
=== why
Regular changes and strong passwords protect the financial data you can access.
=== who
Every user.
=== step: Open Settings → Security | change-password
Click your initials in the top bar and choose **Security**.
=== step: Enter your current password
This confirms it is really you.
=== step: Enter and confirm the new password
The strength meter helps you choose. The policy requires at least the minimum length (10 by default) with uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol, and rejects common passwords and passwords that contain your name or email.
=== step: Click Update password
The new password applies immediately.
=== example: A strong passphrase
Instead of `Partner@123`, which is common and easy to guess, use a passphrase such as `Monsoon-Ledger-47-Kite!`.
=== result
Your password is changed. Other devices where you ticked “Keep me signed in” are signed out.
=== notes
- Never share your password, including with administrators. They can set a temporary password if needed; they never need yours.
- The ERP never stores banking passwords, PINs or OTPs. Do not put them in any field.
=== mistakes
- Reusing a password from another website.
- Choosing a password that contains your name or email; it will be rejected.
=== faq: Why was I sent to the password page right after signing in?
Your account has a temporary password (new account or administrator reset). Set your own password to continue.
