summary: Move money between your own accounts; both sides post together under one transfer reference.
=== what
An internal transfer moves money from one of your accounts to another, for example from the central funding account to a business account.
=== why
Transfers keep both balances right in one step. They are not income or expense and are left out of revenue and spending figures.
=== who
**bank_accounts.manage_transfers** to create; **approve_transfers** or **reject_transfers** to decide.
=== step: Create the transfer | bank-transfer-form
Go to **Internal Transfers → New transfer**. Choose the source and destination accounts, the date, amount, reference and purpose. The form shows the source's available balance.
=== step: Approve and post | bank-transfer-show
With the *Internal transfer* workflow on, the transfer waits for approval. Once approved, a debit on the source and a credit on the destination are posted together with the same TRF number.
=== result
Two linked ledger entries, one on each account.
=== notes
- The source and destination must be different, active accounts in the same currency.
- The source balance is checked and locked while posting. A transfer that would make it negative is refused unless overdraft allows it.
- Reversing a transfer reverses both sides together.
=== mistakes
- Recording a transfer as a withdrawal plus a deposit. Use a transfer so it is not counted as money paid and received.
