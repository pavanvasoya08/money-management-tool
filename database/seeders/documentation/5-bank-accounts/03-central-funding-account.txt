summary: Designate the one account that receives partner capital and funds the business accounts; changing it is controlled.
=== what
The central funding account is the single account partners pay their capital into. Money then moves to the business accounts by internal transfer.
=== why
Tracking one central account shows clearly how much partner money arrived, how much was sent to each business account and how much is still available.
=== who
Designating or removing it needs **bank_accounts.manage_central_account**.
=== step: See the central account | bank-profile
The central account carries a **Central** badge. Its overview tab shows contributions received (linked to Partners & Capital), transfers out, transfers back in, the current balance and the available balance after pending transfers.
=== step: Designate an account | bank-central-settings
Open an active account, go to the **Settings** tab, enter a reason and click **Designate as central funding account**. If the *Bank account change* workflow is on, the change waits for approval.
=== result
Only one account is central at a time. When the new designation takes effect, the previous account loses it automatically and both changes are recorded in the history.
=== notes
- Only active accounts can be designated.
- Designating an account does not move any money and does not change capital or profit-sharing.
=== mistakes
- Recording partner capital straight into a business account. That is allowed, but the central account's "received" figure will then not include it.
