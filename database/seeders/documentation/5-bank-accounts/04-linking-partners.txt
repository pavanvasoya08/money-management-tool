summary: Record which partners hold, jointly hold, operate or benefit from an account, with dates and history.
=== what
A partner mapping records how a partner relates to an account: account holder, joint holder, authorised operator, beneficiary or other. One partner can be linked to many accounts and one account to many partners.
=== why
It shows who can operate each account and lets partner logins see the accounts they are linked to.
=== who
Users with **bank_accounts.manage_partners**.
=== step: Add a mapping | bank-partners
On the account's **Partners** tab choose the partner, the relationship and the **From** date (optionally a **To** date), then click **Add**.
=== step: End a mapping | bank-partners
Click **End mapping** and give the end date and reason. The mapping stays in the list as history.
=== step: See it from the partner's side | bank-partner-tab
A partner's profile has a **Bank accounts** tab listing current and ended links. A partner's own login sees the same tab, with numbers masked.
=== result
Each account shows its current and past partners with dates.
=== notes
- **A mapping never changes ownership, capital share or profit share.** Those are managed under Partners & Capital.
- The same partner cannot have two active mappings with the same relationship on one account.
=== mistakes
- Deleting and re-adding a mapping to correct a date. End it instead so the history stays accurate.
