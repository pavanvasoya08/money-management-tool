=== what
The bank dashboard (**Bank Management → Bank Dashboard**) summarises the bank accounts you are allowed to see: how many there are in each status, opening and current balances, money received and paid, internal transfers, the central funding account and recent activity.
=== why
It answers "how much money is where?" at a glance and shows what needs attention: entries waiting for approval or posting, and accounts that have not been reconciled recently.
=== who
Users with **bank_accounts.view_dashboard**. Every figure respects your record scope: an accountant assigned two accounts sees totals for those two only.
=== step: Read the account tiles | bank-dashboard
The **Accounts** card counts accounts by status and shows opening balances, current balances, money received, money paid and internal transfers. Click a status tile to open the matching list.
=== step: Check the central funding account | bank-dashboard
The **Central joint funding account** card shows what partners deposited, what was transferred out to business accounts, what came back, the current balance and the balance still available after pending transfers.
=== step: Filter the dashboard | bank-dashboard
Narrow every figure by bank, account type, status, partner, GST registration, business activity or date range, then click **Apply**.
=== step: Review recent activity | bank-dashboard-lists
Lower on the page are the account-wise balance summary, the latest bank transactions and transfers, and the accounts whose reconciliation is pending or overdue.
=== result
You see the current position of every account you can access, with links into each account, transaction and transfer.
=== notes
- Balances are always calculated from posted ledger entries. They cannot be typed in.
- Totals in rupees include INR accounts only.
- Partner capital deposited into the bank counts as money received. It is never treated as revenue.
- The charts need an internet connection to load the chart library. Each chart has **Show as table** with the same figures.
=== mistakes
- Expecting draft or pending entries in the balances. Only posted entries change a balance; pending ones show as "awaiting approval or posting".
