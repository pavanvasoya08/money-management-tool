=== what
Reconciliation compares an account's imported statement lines with its posted ledger entries for a period.
=== why
It proves the ledger agrees with the bank and finds missing, wrong or duplicated entries.
=== who
**bank_accounts.manage_reconciliation**; reopening needs **reopen_reconciliation**.
=== step: Start a reconciliation
On **Reconciliation**, choose the account, the period and the imported statement, then click **Start**.
=== step: Accept suggested matches | bank-reconciliation
Suggestions pair lines and entries with the same amount and direction within a few days, ranked higher when references agree. Tick the ones that are right and click **Accept selected matches**.
=== step: Handle what is left | bank-reconciliation-lists
Match the remaining lines manually, **Record missing entry** for lines the ledger lacks, or **Ignore line** with a reason. Ledger entries not on the statement are listed separately. **Unmatch** undoes a match.
=== step: Complete
When every line is matched or ignored, click **Complete reconciliation**. If the ledger and statement closing balances differ, a note explaining why is required.
=== result
A completed reconciliation recording who reconciled it and when.
=== notes
- Reconciliation never changes ledger entries.
- Each line and each entry can be in only one active match.
- Reopening a completed reconciliation can require approval and is always audited.
