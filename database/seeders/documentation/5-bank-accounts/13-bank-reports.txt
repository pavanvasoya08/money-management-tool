=== what
**Bank Reports** offers: bank account summary, account-wise balances, transactions, deposits and withdrawals, internal transfers, partner-linked accounts, GST-wise accounts, monthly cash flow, reconciliation, unmatched statement lines, and bank charges and interest.
=== who
Bank view access; exporting needs **bank_accounts.export**.
=== step: Choose a report | bank-reports
Click a report card.
=== step: Filter, export or print | bank-report-transactions
Set the filters and click **Apply**, then **Export** as CSV, Excel or PDF, or **Print**.
=== result
A report of the accounts and entries you are allowed to see.
=== notes
- Record scope and field rules apply to the screen and to every export: masked fields stay masked and hidden fields are left out.
- Account numbers are always masked in reports.
