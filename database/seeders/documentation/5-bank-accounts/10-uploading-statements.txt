summary: Keep each period's bank statement with its balances; CSV statements can then be imported for reconciliation.
=== what
A statement record keeps the file your bank gave you, the period it covers, its opening and closing balances and its import status.
=== why
Statements are the evidence that reconciliation compares the ledger against.
=== who
**bank_accounts.manage_statements** to upload or archive; **download_documents** to download files.
=== step: Upload a statement | bank-statements
On **Statements**, choose the account, the period, the opening and closing balances from the statement and the file, then click **Upload**. PDF, image, spreadsheet and CSV files up to 10 MB are accepted.
=== result
The statement is listed with its period and status. CSV files can be imported; other files are kept as documents.
=== notes
- The period cannot end in the future.
- Files are stored outside the public web folder and every download is logged.
- This system has no connection to any bank. Statements are always uploaded by a user.
