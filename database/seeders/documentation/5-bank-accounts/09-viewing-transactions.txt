summary: Find entries, read an account's ledger with running balances, and correct posted entries by reversal.
=== what
**Bank Management → Transactions** lists every entry you can see. Each account's **Transactions** tab shows its ledger with opening and closing balances for each entry.
=== why
The ledger is the single source of truth for balances, so every figure can be traced to entries.
=== who
Anyone with bank view access. Reversing needs **bank_accounts.reverse_transactions**.
=== step: Search and filter | bank-transactions
Filter by account, type, status, direction, dates, partner or GST registration. Export the list if you have the export permission.
=== step: Read an account ledger | bank-account-ledger
On the account's **Transactions** tab, each posted entry shows the balance before and after it.
=== step: Reverse a posted entry | bank-transaction-reversed
Open the entry, click **Reverse**, give a reason and date. A new reversal entry cancels it; the original is marked **reversed** and both stay visible.
=== result
Every change to a balance is explained by an entry in the ledger.
=== notes
- Statuses: draft, pending approval, approved, rejected, posted and reversed.
- Drafts can be edited or cancelled. Posted entries are never edited or deleted.
- An entry can be reversed only once.
