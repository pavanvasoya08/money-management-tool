summary: Mark which GST registrations and business activities an account serves, with a primary designation and dates.
=== what
A GST mapping links an account to a GST registration as **primary** or **secondary**. An activity mapping links it to a business activity from the shared master, such as marketplace settlements or supplier payments.
=== why
Reports can then group accounts and money by GSTIN or activity, and the GST registration's page lists the accounts used for it.
=== who
**bank_accounts.manage_gst_mappings** for GST, **bank_accounts.manage_business_mappings** for activities.
=== step: Add a GST mapping | bank-gst
On the **GST** tab choose the registration, primary or secondary, and the effective dates, then click **Add**. Making an account primary for a registration turns the previous primary into secondary.
=== step: Add a business activity | bank-activities
On the **Activities** tab choose the activity and the effective dates, then click **Add**.
=== result
The account shows its current and past GST and activity links; the GST registration's overview lists it.
=== notes
- Only registrations you are allowed to see can be chosen.
- A GST registration referenced by an account or by bank entries cannot be archived.
- Mappings say what an account is used for. They do not split money between GSTINs.
