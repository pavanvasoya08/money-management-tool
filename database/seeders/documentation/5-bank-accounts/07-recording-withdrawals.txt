summary: Record money paid out of an account; the balance is checked and approval can be required.
=== what
A withdrawal records money paid out of an account. **Bank charges** are recorded from **Other entry** and follow the same rules.
=== why
Posted withdrawals reduce the balance and appear on reports and in reconciliation.
=== who
**bank_accounts.create_withdrawal** (or **manage_transactions**).
=== step: Fill in the form | bank-withdrawal-form
Click **Record withdrawal**, choose the account, date and amount, and add the reference and counterparty. A partner's approved capital withdrawal can be linked in **Capital entry**.
=== step: Approval | bank-approval
When the *Bank withdrawal* workflow is on, the entry waits in **Approvals**. Approvers need **bank_accounts.approve_transactions**.
=== result
A posted withdrawal that lowers the balance.
=== notes
- A withdrawal that would make the balance negative is refused unless the account allows an overdraft, and then only up to its limit.
- If the balance has changed by the time an approved entry posts, it stays **approved** with the reason shown and can be posted later.
- Withdrawals are not allowed from frozen or closed accounts.
