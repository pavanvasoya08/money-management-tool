summary: Who can see and do what in Bank Management, and how account numbers and balances are protected.
=== what
Bank Management uses the Control Center: module permissions for each action, user-specific overrides, record scope, assigned accounts, field rules and approval workflows.
=== who
Super Admins manage these in the **Control Center** and **Bank settings**.
=== step: Grant permissions by role | bank-permissions
**Control Center → Action permissions** lists every Bank Accounts action. Grant them to roles in **Module permissions** (choose the role, tick the actions, **Save permissions**). User overrides can allow or deny single actions for one person.
=== step: Limit which accounts a user sees | bank-assigned-view
Give a user *assigned* scope and assign accounts in their access settings. They then see only those accounts in lists, reports, exports and the dashboard; other accounts answer "not found".
=== step: Workflows and rules | bank-settings
**Bank settings** holds account types, the duplicate, matching and overdue rules, and links to the bank approval workflows. Every workflow is off until a Super Admin switches it on.
=== notes
- By default accountants record entries on assigned accounts, managers view all accounts, and partner logins see only accounts they are linked to.
- Field rules can mask or hide balances, IFSC, holder name, counterparty and notes, including in exports.
- The full account number needs **view_full_account_number**, and each reveal is logged. Audit logs never contain full numbers.
- **Never store internet-banking passwords, ATM or UPI PINs, OTPs or card credentials.** The system has no field for them.
