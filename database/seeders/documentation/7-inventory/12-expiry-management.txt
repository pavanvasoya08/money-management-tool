summary: Spot near-expiry and expired batches, keep expired stock out of normal issues, and write it off.
=== what
Products with expiry tracking record an expiry date on every batch. Batches expiring within the warning period are flagged *Near expiry*; past their date they are *Expired*.
=== who
Anyone with **inventory.view**; the warning period and blocking are set with **inventory.manage_inventory_settings**.
=== step: Watch expiry | inv-expiry-report
**Inventory → Inventory Reports → Expiry report** lists expired and near-expiry batches with quantity and days left. The dashboard and the Batches page flag them too.
=== step: Set the rules | inv-settings
**Inventory → Inventory Settings** holds the expiry warning period, whether expired stock is blocked from dispatch and the removal method (FEFO picks the earliest expiry first).
=== result
Expired stock is not issued by mistake and is easy to find.
=== notes
- Expired stock is not counted as available.
- Remove expired stock with a stock outward of type **Expired stock**; issuing it for other purposes needs an approved override.
