summary: Value stock with FIFO or weighted average cost, see cost layers and history, and revalue through approval.
=== what
Stock is valued with **weighted average cost** (each receipt updates the average) or **FIFO** (each receipt is a cost layer and issues consume the oldest layers first). The company default is set in settings; a product can use its own method. Amounts are exact decimals — no accounting entries are created in this phase.
=== who
**inventory.view_stock_cost** to see values; **inventory.manage_stock_valuation** to change the method, revalue and reconcile.
=== step: See the valuation | inv-valuation
**Inventory → Valuation** shows quantity, average and last cost and value per item and warehouse, with pending revaluations and the valuation history. **Reconcile with ledger** checks the summaries against the ledger.
=== step: FIFO layers and revaluation | inv-product-valuation
A product's **Valuation** tab shows its value per warehouse, open FIFO cost layers and history. **Revalue** requests a new unit cost with a reason; it is applied after approval.
=== result
Stock values are consistent with the ledger and ready for the future accounting phase.
=== notes
- The default method is changed in **Inventory Settings** with a reason; a product's method is locked while it has stock.
- Values follow your field permissions in pages, reports and exports.
