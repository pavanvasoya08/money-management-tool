=== what
The inventory dashboard summarises stock in the warehouses you have access to: inventory products and variants, active warehouses, available, reserved, damaged and expired stock, inventory value, low-stock and out-of-stock products, pending transfers and adjustments, stock by warehouse, expiring batches, recent stock movements and recent inventory activity.
=== who
Anyone with **inventory.view_dashboard**. Values need **inventory.view_stock_cost** and follow your field permissions; the ledger list needs **inventory.view_stock_ledger**.
=== step: Open the dashboard | inv-dashboard
Open **Inventory → Inventory Dashboard**. Narrow it with the filters: warehouse, product, variant, category, brand, batch, GST registration, business activity, stock status and a date range (the date range applies to the received / issued totals and the recent movements). Each tile opens the matching list.
=== step: Read the lists | inv-dashboard-lists
Below the tiles: stock by warehouse, items at or below their reorder level, batches near or past expiry, the items with the highest value, recent stock movements and recent inventory activity. Alerts above the lists point to documents waiting for approval, approved documents that could not be posted, and transfers in transit.
=== result
You see the real stock position of your warehouses without opening each one.
=== notes
- Quantity tiles add up different units (pieces, kilograms …) and are indicative; per-item figures are on **Warehouse Stock**.
- **Available** = sellable stock minus reservations, expired batches and batches on hold. Damaged and inspection stock is never counted as available.
- A user whose inventory visibility is *assigned warehouses* sees only those warehouses everywhere, including on this dashboard.
