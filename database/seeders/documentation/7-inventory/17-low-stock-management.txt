summary: Watch items at or below their reorder level and see a suggested order quantity with the preferred supplier.
=== what
Items whose free stock is at or below their reorder level, minimum or safety stock are *Low*; items without free stock are *Out of stock*. Levels are set per product, and per warehouse or variant when needed.
=== who
Anyone with **inventory.view**; levels need **inventory.manage_reorder_settings**.
=== step: Review low stock | inv-low-stock
**Inventory → Low Stock** lists low, out-of-stock and negative items with available quantity, levels, a suggested order quantity and the preferred supplier from Supplier Management. Export it for purchasing.
=== result
You know what to reorder and from whom.
=== notes
- Suggested order = up to the maximum (or reorder level + reorder quantity) minus free stock.
- No purchase order is created; purchasing comes with Purchase Management.
