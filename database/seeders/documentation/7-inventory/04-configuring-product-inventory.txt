summary: Decide how each product's stock is kept — per product or per variant, in which unit, with which levels, tracking and valuation method.
=== what
Inventory uses the products and variants from Product Management; nothing is duplicated. A product tracks stock when **Track inventory** is on in Product Management (services never do). Its inventory page shows stock and holds its inventory settings.
=== who
Settings need **inventory.manage_inventory_settings**, reorder levels **inventory.manage_reorder_settings**, unit conversions **inventory.manage_units**.
=== step: Open the product | inv-products
**Inventory → Product Inventory** lists products and variants that track stock with on-hand, available, reserved and reorder figures. Open one to see its eight tabs: Stock summary, Warehouse-wise stock, Stock ledger, Batches, Serial numbers, Reservations, Valuation and History (tabs for batches and serials appear when that tracking is on).
=== step: Set the inventory settings | inv-product-settings
On **Stock summary → Inventory settings** choose whether stock is kept per product or per variant, the default warehouse, the inventory status, reorder level and quantity, minimum, maximum and safety stock, batch / expiry / serial tracking, negative stock and the valuation method.
=== step: Levels per warehouse and unit conversions | inv-product-units
Add reorder levels for a single warehouse or variant — the most specific one applies. Under **Unit conversions** add conversions for this product, such as 1 box = 12 pieces, so documents can be entered in boxes while stock is kept in pieces.
=== result
Every document for the product is checked against these settings, and low-stock monitoring uses its levels.
=== notes
- The stock unit is the product's unit from Product Management. It and the tracking level cannot change once stock has moved.
- Batch / serial tracking and the valuation method are locked while stock is on hand.
- **Negative stock** is off by default: issues larger than the free stock are refused.
