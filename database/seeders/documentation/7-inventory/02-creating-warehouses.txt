summary: Add warehouses of any type, link them to GST registrations and business activities, assign users, and deactivate or archive them safely.
=== what
A warehouse is any place where you keep stock — your own warehouse, a retail store, a fulfilment or marketplace warehouse, or a virtual location for returns or damaged goods. Warehouse types are an editable list in **Inventory → Settings → Warehouse types**.
=== who
Adding needs **inventory.create_warehouse**, editing **inventory.edit_warehouse**, status, mappings and user assignment **inventory.manage_warehouses**, archiving **inventory.archive_warehouse**.
=== step: Open the list | inv-warehouses
**Inventory → Warehouses** lists your warehouses with type, city, locations, products in stock, stock value and status. Filter by type, status, state, GST registration or business activity.
=== step: Add a warehouse | inv-warehouse-create
Click **Add warehouse**. Enter the name and type; leave the code empty to generate one. Add the address, contact person, mobile and email. Switch on **Storage locations are required for every receipt** if every receipt must name a rack, shelf or bin.
=== step: Link GST registrations and activities | inv-warehouse-overview
The warehouse page has nine tabs: Overview, Stock, Locations, Inward, Outward, Transfers, Adjustments, Documents and History. On **Overview**, link the company GST registrations (from GST Management — never duplicated) and business activities the warehouse operates under, with effective dates.
=== step: Assign users | inv-warehouse-users
Under **Assigned users**, assign the people who work there. Users whose inventory visibility is *assigned warehouses* then see only these warehouses in every list, report and export.
=== result
The warehouse can receive and issue stock, and its stock, documents and history are on its page.
=== notes
- **Deactivate** stops all stock movements until it is activated again (a reason is required).
- **Archive** is possible only when the warehouse holds no stock and has nothing open (in-transit transfers, pending documents, open reservations). History stays readable.
- **Delete** is offered only for an archived warehouse that was never used; a warehouse with any stock or transaction history is never deleted.
- Contact details and internal notes follow your field permissions.
