summary: Hold free stock for an order or requirement so it is not issued elsewhere, then fulfil, release or let it expire.
=== what
A reservation holds stock in a warehouse for a reference — a sales order, an internal requirement or another authorised reference. Reserved stock is not available for other issues. Statuses: Active, Partially fulfilled, Fulfilled, Released, Expired and Cancelled.
=== who
**inventory.manage_reservations** to reserve; **inventory.release_reservations** to release or cancel.
=== step: See reservations | inv-reservations
**Inventory → Reservations** lists open reservations (or any status) with reference, warehouse, dates and items. **Reserve stock** creates one: choose the warehouse, reference type and number, expiry date and the items.
=== step: Release or cancel | inv-reservation
On a reservation, **Release** frees the outstanding quantity when it is no longer needed; **Cancel** is for one created in error. A stock outward line can fulfil a reservation.
=== result
Reserved quantities are excluded from available stock until fulfilled, released or expired.
=== notes
- Only free stock can be reserved, and the same reference cannot reserve the same item twice.
- Reservations past their expiry date expire automatically.
- Sales orders will reserve stock automatically once Sales Management is added.
