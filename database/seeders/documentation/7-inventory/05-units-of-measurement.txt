summary: Manage the list of units and the conversions that let you enter quantities in boxes, cartons or kilograms.
=== what
Units (pieces, boxes, cartons, kilograms, grams, litres, millilitres, metres and your own) are shared with Product Management. Conversions such as 1 carton = 12 pieces let documents be entered in another unit; stock is always kept in the product's own unit.
=== who
**inventory.manage_units**.
=== step: Manage units and conversions | inv-units
Open **Inventory → Inventory Settings → Units & conversions**. Add a unit with its code, name, type and decimal places, or edit one. Below, add a company-wide conversion: *1 × unit = factor of other unit*. Product-specific conversions are added on the product's inventory page.
=== result
Documents accept quantities in any unit that converts to the product's stock unit, and the quantity is converted exactly.
=== notes
- A conversion that would create a loop or a second path between two units is refused.
- Ending a conversion does not change documents already recorded.
- A unit used by products stays active and keeps its code; built-in units keep their code too.
