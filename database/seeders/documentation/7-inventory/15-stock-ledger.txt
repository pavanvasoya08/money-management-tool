summary: Every stock change as a permanent ledger movement — the single source of truth for quantities and values.
=== what
Every posted document creates movements in the stock ledger: date, product, variant, warehouse, location, batch, condition, movement type, quantity in or out, unit cost, value change, source document, who posted and who approved it. Stock balances are summaries kept in step with the ledger.
=== who
**inventory.view_stock_ledger**; values need **inventory.view_stock_cost**.
=== step: Search the ledger | inv-ledger
Open **Inventory → Stock Ledger** and filter by warehouse, location, product, variant, movement type, condition, category, brand, serial number or dates. Export it from the menu.
=== result
Any quantity can be traced back to the documents that produced it.
=== notes
- Posted movements are never edited or deleted; corrections are reversals or adjustments that post new movements.
- Each document can be posted only once, even when two people post at the same moment.
- Pending, rejected and cancelled documents never create movements.
- **Valuation → Reconcile with ledger** checks every balance against the ledger.
