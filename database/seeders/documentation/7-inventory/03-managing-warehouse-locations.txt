summary: Organise each warehouse into zones, racks, shelves and bins, and see the stock each location holds.
=== what
Each warehouse can have any number of storage locations nested up to six levels — zone, section, aisle, rack, shelf and bin. Stock can be received into, issued from and moved between locations.
=== who
**inventory.manage_locations**. Anyone with **inventory.view** can see locations and their stock.
=== step: Add locations | inv-locations
Open **Inventory → Storage Locations** (or a warehouse's Locations tab) and choose the warehouse. Enter a code and name, choose the type and the location it sits **Inside**, and optionally a capacity and description. The list shows the tree with the stock and number of products in each location.
=== result
Receipts, issues, transfers and counts can name the exact location, and the stock ledger and reports show stock per location.
=== notes
- A location cannot be placed inside itself or inside one of its own sub-locations.
- **Deactivate** stops new stock going into a location; **Archive** is refused while it holds stock or has active sub-locations. Locations are never deleted, so their history stays.
- When a warehouse requires locations, every receipt line must name one.
