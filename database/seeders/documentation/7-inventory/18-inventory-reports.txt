summary: Twenty inventory reports with filters and CSV, Excel or PDF export — limited to your warehouses and permissions.
=== what
Reports: complete stock summary, warehouse-wise, product-wise and variant-wise stock, stock ledger, inward, outward, transfers, adjustments, opening stock, batch-wise stock, expiry, serial numbers, reserved stock, available stock, low stock with suggested reorder, out-of-stock, valuation, stock movement summary and damaged stock.
=== who
Anyone with **inventory.view**; export needs **inventory.export**; cost and value columns need **inventory.view_stock_cost**.
=== step: Choose a report | inv-reports
Open **Inventory → Inventory Reports** and pick a report.
=== step: Filter and export | inv-report-summary
Filter by dates, warehouse, location, product, variant, category, brand, batch, serial number, movement type, status, GST registration and business activity (where they apply). **Export** downloads exactly what you see as CSV, Excel or PDF; every export is logged.
=== result
You get reliable stock figures for any slice of your inventory.
=== notes
- Reports cover only the warehouses you may see, and value columns follow your field permissions.
- Totals that add different units are indicative only.
