summary: Map activities to registrations and maintain the configurable master lists.
=== what
Business activities (e-commerce, wholesale, retail and any you add) describe what each registration is used for. The masters hold states and union territories, registration types, partner relationship types and business activities.
=== why
Reports and future modules filter registrations by activity, and consistent master lists keep data clean.
=== who
Mapping activities needs **manage_business_mappings**; maintaining masters needs **manage_masters** (Super Admin by default).
=== step: Map an activity | gst-activities
On **Business activities**, choose the activity and its effective dates, then click **Add**. Use **Edit** to change dates, status or notes.
=== step: Maintain masters | gst-masters
Open **Masters & rules**. Each tab lists entries with how many records use them. Add an entry, or edit one to rename it or deactivate it.
=== step: Business rules | gst-rules
The **Business rules** tab holds the single-owner rule and how many days ahead expiry warnings appear.
=== result
Registrations carry accurate activity information, and the lists stay under control.
=== notes
- The code of an entry that is in use cannot change; deactivate it instead.
- State codes must be the official two-digit codes. They are pre-loaded and rarely need changes.
- Approval requirements are set in **Control Center → Approval workflows**.
=== mistakes
- Adding a near-duplicate activity ("Ecommerce" and "E-commerce"). Edit the existing one instead.
