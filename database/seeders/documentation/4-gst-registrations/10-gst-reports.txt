summary: Seven reports with filters, sorting and CSV, Excel and PDF export, all limited to what you may see.
=== what
Seven reports: **All registrations, Active registrations, Cancelled and suspended, State-wise summary, Registration type summary, Partner-wise registrations** and **Recently updated**.
=== why
Reports answer common questions (which states, which partners, what changed) in a printable, exportable form.
=== who
Viewing needs **gst_registrations.view**; exporting needs **export**; printing needs **print**.
=== step: Choose a report | gst-reports
Open **Registration reports** and pick one.
=== step: Filter and export | gst-report-partner
Filter by search text, state, type, status, partner and dates, choose the sort order, then **Export** as CSV, Excel or PDF, or **Print**.
=== step: Export the register | gst-list
The **Registrations** list has its own filters and an **Export** menu for the filtered list.
=== result
A report or file containing only the registrations and fields you are allowed to see.
=== notes
- Record scope and field rules apply on screen and in every export.
- Archived registrations are excluded unless you choose **With archived**.
- Exports are logged in the activity log.
