summary: Upload certificates and supporting documents securely; preview, download and archive with history.
=== what
The **Documents** tab stores the registration certificate, amendment and cancellation certificates, proof of place of business, authorisations and other supporting documents.
=== why
Keeping the certificate next to the record lets anyone with access check details quickly.
=== who
Uploading and archiving need **manage_documents**; downloading needs **download_documents**.
=== step: Upload | gst-documents
Choose the **document type**, optionally a title, expiry date and description, choose the file and click **Upload**.
=== step: Preview or download
**Preview** opens PDFs and images in the browser; the download button saves the original file. Each view and download is logged.
=== step: Archive
Click **Archive**, give a reason (for example "replaced by amended certificate") and confirm. Use **Show archived documents** to see the history.
=== result
Current documents are listed; archived ones stay available as history.
=== notes
- Allowed types: PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, DOCX and XLSX, up to 5 MB. The file content is checked, not just the extension.
- Files are stored outside the public web folder under random names.
- Documents with an expiry date appear on the dashboard before they expire.
=== mistakes
- Uploading a screenshot of the portal login page or anything containing passwords or OTPs. Never upload credentials.
