summary: Link partners to registrations with a relationship type and dates; mappings keep their history.
=== what
A mapping says how a partner relates to a registration: owner, proprietor, partner, authorised representative, business associate or other. One partner can be mapped to many registrations, and one registration to many partners.
=== why
It shows who is responsible for each registration and lets partner logins see the registrations they are connected to.
=== who
Users with **gst_registrations.manage_partners**.
=== step: Add a mapping | gst-partners
On **Partner associations**, choose the partner, the relationship, the **From** date and optionally the **To** date, then click **Add**.
=== step: End a mapping
Click **End mapping**, give the end date and reason. The mapping stays in the list as history.
=== step: See it from the partner side | gst-partner-tab
A partner's profile has a **GST registrations** tab listing current and ended associations. Partner logins see the same tab for themselves.
=== step: Optional single-owner rule | gst-rules
A Super Admin can switch on **Each GST registration may have only one active owner** in **Masters & rules → Business rules**.
=== result
Each registration shows its current and past partners with dates.
=== notes
- **A mapping does not change capital or profit-sharing.** Ownership shares are managed under Partners & Capital.
- The same partner cannot have two active mappings to one registration at the same time.
- Mappings of cancelled or archived registrations are read-only.
=== mistakes
- Deleting and re-adding to "correct" a date. End the mapping instead, so the history stays correct.
