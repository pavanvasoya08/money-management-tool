=== what
Adding a registration records the GSTIN, legal and trade name, registration type and dates, contact details and the principal place of business.
=== why
Every registration the business holds should exist exactly once so other records can refer to it.
=== who
Users with **gst_registrations.create**.
=== prerequisites
- The registration certificate (Form GST REG-06) in front of you.
- The state and registration type exist in **Masters & rules** (all official state codes are pre-loaded).
=== step: Open the form | gst-create
In **Registrations** click **Add GST registration**. Type the **GSTIN**; it is checked as you type and the **State** fills in from the first two digits. Choose the **Registration type** and enter the **Legal business name** exactly as on the certificate.
=== step: Fill in the remaining sections | gst-create-address
Section A also takes the trade name, business constitution, registration status, registration date and effective date. Section B is the **principal place of business** (address line 1, city, state and PIN code are required). Section C holds contact details, the portal reference (e.g. the ARN) and internal notes.
=== step: Save
Click **Add registration**. The registration starts as **Pending verification**. If the *New GST registration* approval workflow is active, it waits in the approval inbox; otherwise the status you chose applies at once.
=== step: Approve (if required) | gst-approval
An approver opens **Approvals**, reviews the requested values and approves or rejects. Approval applies the requested status; rejection leaves the registration as a rejected request that can be corrected and resubmitted, or archived.
=== example: A new state registration (illustrative)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| GSTIN | 24AAKFM4821R1ZS |
| Registration type | Regular |
| Legal name | Mehta Traders |
| Trade name | Mehta Home Store |
| Registration date | 01 Apr 2025 |
| Principal place | 14, Shreeji Complex, CG Road, Ahmedabad 380009 |

Result: a Gujarat (24) registration, pending until approved, then **Active**.
=== result
The registration appears in the register with its principal place of business, default settings and a first history entry.
=== notes
- Duplicate GSTINs are refused, whatever the letter case.
- Choose **Pending verification** until you have checked the certificate.
- Casual and non-resident registrations show a **validity end date** field.
=== mistakes
- Typing the trade name in the legal name field.
- Entering portal usernames or passwords in notes. Never store credentials.
=== faq: Can I add the same GSTIN twice for two branches?
No. One GSTIN is one registration. Add the branch as an **additional place of business** on the Locations tab.
