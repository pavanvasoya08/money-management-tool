summary: Move a registration through its lifecycle; cancellations may need approval; archived records are kept.
=== what
A registration is **Pending verification, Active, Inactive, Suspended** or **Cancelled**. Separately, it can be **archived**, which hides it from day-to-day lists without deleting it.
=== why
The status tells users and later modules whether a registration can be used.
=== who
Each action has its own permission: **activate, deactivate, suspend, cancel, archive**.
=== step: Change the status | gst-status-menu
Click **Change status** on the profile and choose the new status. Suspension and cancellation need a reason; cancellation also takes the cancellation date.
=== step: Cancellation approval
If the *GST cancellation* workflow is active, a cancellation waits for approval and the registration stays as it is until approved.
=== step: Archive or restore
An inactive or cancelled registration can be archived with a reason. Archived records are read-only; **Restore** brings them back.
=== result
Every change is recorded in the status history with who, when, the effective date and the reason.
=== notes
- Cancelled is final: a cancelled registration cannot be reactivated. Add a new registration if the business registers again.
- Registrations are **never deleted**. A registration that other records refer to cannot even be archived.
- The default registration must be changed before it can be archived.
=== mistakes
- Archiving an active registration that is still in use. Deactivate or cancel it first.
