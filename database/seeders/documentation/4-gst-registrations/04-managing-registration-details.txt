=== what
Each registration has a profile with eight tabs: **Overview, Registration details, Partner associations, Business activities, Business locations, Documents, Settings** and **History**.
=== why
All information about a registration lives in one place with its history.
=== who
Viewing needs **gst_registrations.view**; editing needs **edit**; settings need **manage_settings**; the History tab needs **view_history**.
=== step: Open the profile | gst-profile
Click a GSTIN in the list. The left card shows the GSTIN, status and key facts, with the edit button and **Change status**.
=== step: Review and edit details | gst-details
The **Registration details** tab shows sections A–C. Click the pencil to edit.
=== step: Understand key-detail changes
GSTIN, legal name, PAN, registration type, registration date and effective date are **key details**. When the *GST key-detail change* workflow is active, changing them creates an approval request and the old values stay until it is approved. Other fields save at once.
=== step: Set defaults for later modules | gst-settings
On **Settings**, mark the registration as the **default for business operations** (only one can be), choose the default place of supply, state and registration type, effective dates and tax-related flags. These are stored for future sales and purchase modules; nothing is calculated now.
=== step: Check the history | gst-history
**History** lists status changes, change requests with old and new values, and the audit log.
=== result
Details stay accurate, and every change can be traced.
=== notes
- Only an active, approved registration can be the default.
- Fields hidden or masked by your field permissions appear that way here and in exports.
=== mistakes
- Editing the legal name to fix a typo without a reason. Add the reason so approvers understand the change.
=== faq: Why is my change not visible yet?
It is a key-detail change waiting for approval. The profile shows a banner while it is pending.
