summary: Keep the principal place of business and unlimited additional places up to date.
=== what
Each registration has exactly one active **principal place of business** and any number of **additional places of business** such as godowns, branches or showrooms.
=== why
Addresses on invoices and e-way bills must match the registration.
=== who
Users with **gst_registrations.manage_locations**.
=== step: Add a location | gst-locations
On **Business locations**, open **Add a business location**, fill in the address (line 1, city, state and PIN code are required) and contact details, then save.
=== step: Edit, change the principal or deactivate
Use **Edit** to correct an address, **Make principal** to promote an active location (the old principal becomes an additional place), and **Deactivate** / **Activate** for the others.
=== result
The registration shows all current and former locations.
=== notes
- The principal cannot be deactivated; make another location principal first.
- Locations are never deleted.
- If address fields are masked for you, you can see the location but not edit it.
=== mistakes
- Creating a new registration for a branch in the same state. A branch is an additional place of business under the same GSTIN.
